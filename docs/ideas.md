# Ideas parking lot

> **What this is:** ideas that aren't right for now but are worth keeping. Parking an idea isn't rejecting it; it stops new ideas derailing the current project.
> **Rule:** revisit after the current project ships, not before.

---

## Driving instructor management platform
**Parked:** 05/11/2026

**Idea:** a local marketplace and admin tool for driving instructors, like Booksy for hairdressers. Learners pick an instructor by area, availability and price, and book. Instructors get their day organised: lessons, pupil addresses, payments.

**Why it's interesting:** real paying customers (instructors); clear processes to map; personal link from my own driving lessons.

**Why parked:**
- Crowded UK market: Total Drive, MyDriveTime, Bitepoint, Passly, BookDrive, DriveSync
- AI isn't core, so a poor fit for the AI Innovator award
- Two-sided: needs instructors and learners at once, so it's slow to get real users
- Stores home addresses, which raises its own privacy issues

**Questions to answer before reviving:**
- What do instructors hate about the existing apps? (Read their reviews; talk to 3 instructors)
- Is there a gap competitors miss, e.g. a specific region, independent instructors, or test-readiness tracking?
- Where could AI genuinely be core, e.g. route-aware scheduling or predicting test readiness?
