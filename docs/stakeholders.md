# Stakeholder map

> **What this is:** a list of everyone who affects or is affected by the project, what they care about, and how I keep them involved.
> **Why it matters:** projects usually fail because the wrong people weren't consulted, not because of code. In banks this is a core delivery skill ("stakeholder management").
> **How to read the grid:** *Influence* = how much they can change the project's success. *Interest* = how much they care about it.
