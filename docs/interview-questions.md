# Discovery interview guide

> **What this is:** the script for 15–20 minute interviews with students, used in Phase 1 (Discovery).
> **Goal:** test assumptions A1–A5 in `raid-log.md` by hearing about **real past behaviour**, not opinions about an app that doesn't exist yet.
> **Golden rules (from *The Mom Test*):**
> - Ask about what they **did**, not what they **would** do.
> - **Don't pitch the app** until the very end (or at all).
> - Listen more than you talk. Silence is fine; let them fill it.
> - When something interesting comes up, ask **"Tell me more"** or **"Why was that?"**

## Research questions (what I need to learn)
| ID | Research question | Tests assumption |
|---|---|---|
| RQ1 | How do students currently keep track of their money? | A1, A2 |
| RQ2 | When and why does money cause stress during a term? | A1 |
| RQ3 | How do students decide whether to spend on social things? | A4 |
| RQ4 | Which banks and tools do they use, and would they export a CSV? | A2, A3 |
| RQ5 | How do they feel about AI helping with money? | A5 |

## Before the interview (2 mins)
Read this out:
> "Thanks for doing this. I'm researching how students manage money at uni for a project. There are no right or wrong answers, I'm just interested in your experience. It'll take about 15 minutes. I won't ask for any bank details or exact balances, and my notes will be anonymous. You can skip any question or stop whenever. Is it OK if I take notes?"

Record: participant code (e.g. P01), year of study, living situation (halls / house / home), works part-time? (Y/N). **No names.**

---

## Section 1: Warm-up (2 mins)
1. What's your money situation at uni like, roughly? Where does your money come from?
2. How often do you check your bank balance?

## Section 2: Tracking today (RQ1, RQ4) (4 mins)
3. Talk me through the last time you checked how much money you had. What were you doing, and what did you do next?
4. Do you use anything to keep track: banking app features, a spreadsheet, notes, nothing? What made you start or stop?
5. Which bank(s) do you use?
6. Have you ever exported a statement or looked at your full transaction history? What for?

## Section 3: Stress points (RQ2) (4 mins)
7. Think about last term. Was there a point where money got tight? When was it, and what happened?
8. When your loan came in, how did you plan how long it needed to last? *(Listen for whether they think in months, weeks or terms.)*
9. What do you wish you'd known earlier about where your money was going?

## Section 4: Spending decisions (RQ3) (4 mins)
10. Tell me about the last night out or social thing you almost didn't go to because of money. How did you decide?
11. Have you ever been saving for something specific, like a trip or a gig? How did that go?

## Section 5: AI and trust (RQ5) (2 mins)
12. Have you used AI (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) for anything to do with money? What happened?
13. What would make you trust, or not trust, an AI giving you money advice?

## Wrap-up (1 min)
14. Is there anything about money at uni I haven't asked that I should have?
15. Would you be up for testing a tool later in the term? *(Collect contact details separately from notes, so notes stay anonymous.)*

## Question do's and don'ts (quick reference)
| ❌ Don't ask | ✅ Ask instead |
|---|---|
| "Would you use an app that…?" | "Tell me about the last time you…" |
| "Is budgeting stressful?" | "When did money last stress you out?" |
| "How much do you spend on going out?" | "Can you think of last week? What did you spend on?" |
| "Do you think AI could help?" | "Have you used AI for money? What happened?" |
