# RAID log

> **What this is:** a tracker of **R**isks, **A**ssumptions, **I**ssues and **D**ependencies. Standard on delivery teams in banks.
> - **Risk:** something that *might* go wrong. Track likelihood, impact and a plan.
> - **Assumption:** something I believe is true but haven't proven. Each one needs testing.
> - **Issue:** something that *has* gone wrong and needs fixing now.
> - **Dependency:** something I need from someone or something else.
>
> Review weekly. Rating: High / Medium / Low.
